# Planets (Grahas), Part 2 — Planetary Strengths and Relationships — Jyotisha Reference

Scope: how planetary strength is judged in Jyotisha — exaltation and debilitation, moolatrikona, own sign, directional strength, planetary friendships (natural, temporal, compound), avasthas, aspects, conjunctions and combustion.

Conventions used in this file:
- Degrees are within a sign unless stated otherwise.
- "Nth from X" counts inclusively, with X itself as the 1st.
- "Source note" marks places where the original slides were inconsistent; nothing was silently corrected.

---

## 1. Factors of planetary strength

Planets express their energies with different intensity depending on where they are placed in the zodiac. Because of their constant motion, a planet is at times very strong and at other times weak. The key factors for evaluating strength are:

1. Exaltation / debilitation — Uchcha / Neecha
2. Trinal strength — Moolatrikona
3. Planetary rulership — Swakshetra (own sign)
4. Directional strength — Dig Bala
5. Planetary friendship — friends / enemies / neutrals
6. Planetary state — Avasthas
7. Conjunction — Yuti
8. Combustion — Astanga

### 2.1 Arohi and Avarohi (ascending and descending)

A planet moving from its deep debilitation point towards its deep exaltation point is Arohi; moving from deep exaltation towards deep debilitation it is Avarohi.

| Planet | Arohi span | Avarohi span |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | 11° Libra to 10° Aries | 11° Aries to 10° Libra |
| Moon | 4° Scorpio to 3° Taurus | 4° Taurus to 3° Scorpio |
| Mars | 29° Cancer to 28° Capricorn | 29° Capricorn to 28° Cancer |
| Mercury | 16° Pisces to 15° Virgo | 16° Virgo to 15° Pisces |
| Jupiter | 6° Capricorn to 5° Cancer | 6° Cancer to 5° Capricorn |
| Venus | 28° Virgo to 27° Pisces | 28° Pisces to 27° Virgo |
| Saturn | 21° Aries to 20° Libra | 21° Libra to 20° Aries |
| Rahu | 20° Scorpio to 20° Taurus | 20° Taurus to 20° Scorpio |
| Ketu | 20° Taurus to 20° Scorpio | 20° Taurus to 20° Scorpio (as printed) |

- Planets in Arohi position are considered good.
- A planet in Avarohi position normally loses strength and benefic results and becomes helpless to give good results.

Source note: the slide prints the same span for Ketu in both columns. By the pattern of the other rows, Ketu's Avarohi span would be 20° Scorpio to 20° Taurus.

### 2.2 Uchchabhilashi and Neechabhilashi

- Uchchabhilashi: a planet in the sign immediately preceding its exaltation sign — "desiring to enter its sign of exaltation".
- Neechabhilashi: a planet in the sign immediately preceding its debilitation sign.

Examples:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Aries (10°) | Pisces | Libra (10°) | Virgo |
| Mars | Capricorn (28°) | Sagittarius | Cancer (28°) | Gemini |

## 5. Dig Bala (directional strength)

| House | Direction | Planets strong here | Planets weak here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | East | Mercury, Jupiter | Saturn |
| 4th | North | Moon, Venus | Sun, Mars |
| 7th | West | Saturn | Jupiter, Mercury |
| 10th | South | Sun, Mars | Moon, Venus |

Rationale:
- Jupiter and Mercury are the planets of intelligence and wisdom; early morning (sunrise, the 1st house) is the best time for study and meditation, when the mind is fresh and clear.
- Sun and Mars, the two hottest planets, show their influence best at midday (10th house), the hottest part of the day.
- Saturn, lord of shadow and darkness and the alter ego of the rising Sun, shows his strength best when the Sun sets (7th house).
- Moon and Venus, the two brightest objects in the night sky, are strongest when it is darkest (4th house, midnight).

## 6. Planetary relationships (Maitri)

Relationships between planets are of three kinds: Natural (Naisargika), Temporary (Tatkalika) and Compound (Panchadha).

### 6.1 Natural (permanent) relationships — Naisargika Maitri

Rules:
- These are fixed and based on each planet's moolatrikona sign.
- Lords of the 2nd, 4th, 5th, 8th, 9th and 12th signs from the moolatrikona sign are natural friends.
- Lords of the 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th and 11th signs from the moolatrikona sign are natural enemies.
- If a planet qualifies as both friend and enemy because it owns two signs, it is neutral (Sama).

Worked example — the Sun (moolatrikona sign Leo):
- Friend: the 4th from Leo is Scorpio, lord Mars (friend). Mars's other sign, Aries, is the 9th from Leo (friend). Mars is therefore a friend of the Sun.
- Enemy: the 3rd from Leo is Libra, lord Venus (enemy). Venus's other sign, Taurus, is the 10th from Leo (enemy). Venus is therefore an enemy of the Sun.
- Neutral: the 2nd from Leo is Virgo, lord Mercury (friend). Mercury's other sign, Gemini, is the 11th from Leo (enemy). Mercury is both, so it is neutral to the Sun.

Worked example — the Moon (moolatrikona sign Taurus): friends Sun, Mercury; enemies none; neutral Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn.

|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Moon, Mars, Jupiter | Venus, Saturn | Mercury |
| Moon | Sun, Mercury | — | Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn |
| Mars | Sun, Moon, Jupiter | Mercury | Venus, Saturn |

| Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mars | Mercury, Venus | Saturn |
| Venus | Mercury, Saturn | Sun, Moon | Mars, Jupiter |
| Saturn | Mercury, Venus | Sun, Moon, Mars | Jupiter |
| Rahu | Jupiter, Venus, Saturn | Sun, Moon, Mars | Mercury |
| Ketu | Mars, Venus, Saturn | Sun, Moon | Jupiter, Mercury |

### 6.2 Temporal relationships — Tatkalika Maitri

Rules:
- These are dynamic and depend on the planets' positions in a particular horoscope.
- There are no neutrals: only friends or enemies.
- Planets in the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th and 12th from a planet are its temporal friends.
- Planets in the 1st (same sign), 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th and 9th from a planet are its temporal enemies.

Example chart (Cancer Ascendant):

| Sign | House | Occupants |
|---|---|---|
| Cancer | 1 | Ascendant, Jupiter, Mars |
| Libra | 4 | Saturn |
| Sagittarius | 6 | Rahu |
| Aquarius | 8 | Moon |
| Gemini | 12 | Sun, Mercury, Venus, Ketu |

Sun's temporal relationships in this chart (Sun in Gemini):
- Mercury, Venus, Ketu — in the same sign as the Sun (1st from it): enemies.
- Jupiter, Mars — in Cancer, 2nd from the Sun: friends.
- Saturn — in Libra, 5th from the Sun: enemy.
- Rahu — in Sagittarius, 7th from the Sun: enemy.
- Moon — in Aquarius, 9th from the Sun: enemy.

Temporal relationships of every planet in this example chart:

| Planet | Temporal friends | Temporal enemies |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | Mars, Jupiter | Moon, Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu |
| Moon | Rahu | Sun, Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Ketu |
| Mars | Sun, Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Ketu | Moon, Jupiter, Rahu |
| Mercury | Sun, Moon, Jupiter (as printed) | Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu (as printed) |
| Jupiter | Sun, Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Ketu | Moon, Mars, Rahu |
| Venus | Mars, Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mercury, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu |
| Saturn | Mars, Jupiter, Rahu | Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Ketu |
| Rahu | Moon, Saturn | Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Ketu |
| Ketu | Mars, Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Rahu |

Source notes:
- The slide labels the Sun's companions as "12th House"; that is the chart house (Gemini) the Sun occupies. The other bullets (2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th) are counted from the Sun.
- The Mercury row does not follow from the rule. With Mercury in Gemini, the rule gives friends Mars, Jupiter and enemies Sun, Moon, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu. The compound table in 6.3 is consistent with these rule-derived values, not the printed ones.

### 6.3 Compound relationships — Panchadha Maitri Chakra (five-fold relationship)

The Panchadha Maitri Chakra combines the natural (permanent) and temporal relationships to give the compound relationship. It is one of the most important tools for understanding how planets interact in a horoscope.

| Natural | Temporal | Compound result | Sanskrit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friend | Friend | Close friend | Adhi-Mitra |
| Neutral | Friend | Friend | Mitra |
| Enemy | Friend | Neutral | Sama |
| Friend | Enemy | Neutral | Sama |
| Neutral | Enemy | Enemy | Satru |
| Enemy | Enemy | Bitter enemy | Adhi-Satru |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Mars, Jupiter | — | Moon | Mercury | Venus, Saturn |
| Moon | — | — | Sun, Mercury | Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn | — |
| Mars | Sun | Venus, Saturn | Moon, Mercury, Jupiter | — | — |

| Venus | — | Mars, Jupiter | Mercury, Saturn | — | Sun, Moon |
| Saturn | — | Jupiter | Mars, Mercury | — | Sun, Moon, Venus (as printed) |

Source note: Saturn's natural relationship with Venus is friend and its temporal relationship in this chart is enemy, which by the rule gives neutral, not bitter enemy.

## 8. Avasthas (planetary states)

### 8.1 Baladi Avasthas (age-based states)

There are five: Bala, Kumara, Yuva, Vriddha and Mrita. Each sign is divided into five 6° portions. The order runs forward in odd signs and is reversed in even signs.

| Degrees in sign | Odd signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius) | Even signs (Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces) |
|---|---|---|
| 0°–6° | Balavastha (infant state) | Mritavastha (dead state) |
| 6°–12° | Kumaravastha (youthful state) | Vriddhavastha (aged state) |
| 12°–18° | Yuvavastha (adolescent or young state) | Yuvavastha (adolescent or young state) |
| 18°–24° | Vriddhavastha (aged state) | Kumaravastha (youthful state) |
| 24°–30° | Mritavastha (dead state) | Balavastha (infant state) |

### 8.2 Deeptadi Avasthas (dignity-based states)

| No. | Avastha | Condition | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deepta (bright) | In exaltation sign | Extremely strong | High sense of achievement |
| 2 | Svastha (healthy) | In own sign | Stable and strong | Good health and comforts |
| 3 | Mudita (happy) | In an extreme friend's sign | Joyful and cooperative | Beneficial, harmonious effects |
| 4 | Shanta (peaceful) | In a friend's sign | Calm and relaxed | Gets support from friends; no effort required |
| 5 | Deena (helpless) | In a neutral sign | Weak and struggling | Challenges, struggles, obstacles |
| 6 | Dukhita (sorrowful) | In an enemy's sign | Sorrows and miseries | Unwarranted fights, conflicts, discords |
| 7 | Vikala (in trouble or distress) | Combust by the Sun | Full of anxiety | Uncomfortable and miserable state |
| 8 | Khala (wicked) | In an extreme enemy's sign | Conspiring against enemies | A good-natured person can turn sour when pushed to the limits |
| 9 | Kopa (extremely angry) | Eclipsed by the Sun | Destructive | Brings havoc in life through battles and conflicts |

## 9. Planetary Sambandha (relationships by position)

Four kinds:
1. Aspect (Drishti)
2. Aspecting own house (Sthana Drishti)
3. Conjunction (Graha Yuti)
4. Combustion (Astanga)

### 9.1 Aspect (Drishti)

- An aspect is a planet "looking at" specific houses or planets from its own position.
- All planets aspect the 7th house from themselves and any planets in it.

Special aspects (in addition to the 7th):

| Planet | Houses aspected (counted from the planet) |
|---|---|
| Mars | 4th, 7th, 8th |
| Jupiter | 5th, 7th, 9th |
| Saturn | 3rd, 7th, 10th |
| All other planets | 7th only |

Rahu and Ketu aspects (several views exist):
- View 1: full aspect on the 5th, 7th and 9th from them.
- View 2: full aspect on the 3rd, 7th and 11th from them.

General principles:
- Aspects by natural benefic planets are always good.
- Aspects by natural malefics are always evil.
- Aspects of lords of good houses, such as the 5th and 9th, are good.
- Aspects of lords of evil houses, such as the 6th, 8th and 12th, are evil.

Examples (Taurus Ascendant chart):

| Planet and position | Aspects fall on |
|---|---|
| Mars in Gemini | Virgo (4th), Sagittarius (7th), Capricorn (8th) |
| Jupiter in Aquarius | Gemini (5th), Leo (7th), Libra (9th) |
| Saturn in Capricorn | Pisces (3rd), Cancer (7th), Libra (10th) |
| Sun in Aries | Libra (7th) |
| Venus in Gemini | Sagittarius (7th) |
| Mercury in Sagittarius | Gemini (7th) |

Venus in Gemini and Mercury in Sagittarius aspect each other (mutual 7th aspect).

### 9.2 Aspecting own house (Sthana Drishti)

- When a planet aspects the house it rules, it reinforces and protects that domain of life.

Examples:
- Aquarius Ascendant, Mars in Libra (9th house): Mars's 7th aspect falls on Aries, its own sign (3rd house).
- Sagittarius Ascendant, Jupiter in Aries (5th house): Jupiter's 9th aspect falls on Sagittarius, its own sign (1st house).

### 9.3 Conjunction (Yuti)

- A conjunction is two or more planets posited in one sign or house.
- Benefic conjunction: involves natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon).
- Malefic conjunction: involves natural malefics (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, Sun).
- Conjunctions can create benefic or malefic yogas (unions).

| Conjunction | Yoga | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Sun + Mercury | Budha-Aditya Yoga | Sharp intellect, communication skills |
| Moon + Jupiter | Gajakesari Yoga | Wisdom, prosperity, respect |
| Sun + Saturn | — | Causes friction and enmities |
| Jupiter + Rahu | Guru-Chandala Yoga | Creates an obsession for gaining knowledge and securing wealth |

### 9.4 Combustion (Astanga)

- A planet is combust when it comes too close to the burning rays of the Sun while occupying the same sign as the Sun.
- Combust planets lose much of their positive power and give only superficial benefits.

| Mars | 17° | — |
| Mercury | 14° | 12° |
| Jupiter | 11° | — |
| Venus | 10° | 8° |
| Saturn | 15° | — |
