# Signs (Rasis) — Jyotisha Reference

Scope: the twelve zodiac signs (rasis) in Jyotisha — spans, lords, classifications, significations, the three lagnas (Janma, Chandra, Surya), and sign-to-nakshatra mapping, with worked examples.

Conventions used in this file:
- Signs are numbered 1–12 from Aries. Longitudes are written DD°MM' within a sign, or 0°–360° from 0° Aries.
- A nakshatra entry such as "Krittika (1)" means one pada of that nakshatra falls in the sign.
- "Source note" marks places where the original slides were inconsistent; nothing was silently corrected.

---

## 1. Fundamentals of the rasis

- Rasi comes from a Sanskrit root meaning "heap" or "collection": a heap of stars.
- The natural zodiac has 12 rasis of equal span. Each rasi is 30° (30° × 12 = 360°).
- Each rasi is allotted to a specific planet, called its ruler or lord (adhipati).
- The nakshatras are distributed equally across the rasis by nakshatra pada.
- Positions of planets and nakshatras within a rasi are given as longitudes in degrees: DD:MM:SS.
- Each rasi has a symbol, certain qualities and natural characteristics.
- The natural zodiac chart, with Aries as the first sign, is called the Kalapurusha Kundli.

## 5. Classifications explained

### 5.1 Kalapurusha — the zodiac as a body
- The zodiac represents the body of the Kalapurusha (Time Eternal). Each sign corresponds to a limb (see master table).
- When benefic planets occupy or aspect a sign, they strengthen the limb of the native that corresponds to that sign.
- When malefic planets occupy or aspect a sign, they tend to weaken or cause disease in the corresponding limb.

### 5.2 Purusharthas — the four goals of life
| Purushartha | Meaning | Deals with | Signs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dharma (duty) | Righteousness, law and duty; the ability to take right action, duty to others and oneself | Moral values | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius |
| Artha (material gain) | Wealth and prosperity; goals around work, career and finances | Economic values | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn |
| Kama (physical and sense pleasures) | Desire and pleasure; practical desires and needs; passion — sexual, religious, for life | Psychological values | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius |
| Moksha (release and salvation) | Self-realisation, liberation; enlightenment, transcending physical life to attain higher awareness | Spiritual values | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces |

Moksha is the final goal: self-transcendence.

## 7. The Ascendant (Lagna) — the rising sign

- The Ascendant (Lagna) is the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the exact time of birth.
- It is the most vital point in a birth chart. It defines the physical body, personality and life direction, and is the foundation for interpreting all other houses and planetary influences.
- Also called Udaya Lagna or Janma Lagna.
- Because the Earth rotates once every 24 hours, all 12 signs rise in sequence during that period.
- Each sign takes about 2 hours to rise (24 hours ÷ 12 signs), so the Ascendant cycles through all 12 signs in one day.
- The exact duration of each lagna varies with latitude and season. Near the equator, signs rise more evenly (close to 2 hours each). At higher latitudes, some signs rise faster and others slower. This is why precise birth time and location are essential for accurate lagna calculation.

### 7.1 Signs above and below the horizon
Example with Aries rising:
- Rising sign (Ascendant, house 1): Aries
- Signs below the horizon (houses 2–7): Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra
- Signs above the horizon: Aries, Pisces, Aquarius, Capricorn, Sagittarius, Scorpio

## 8. The Moon sign (Chandra Lagna)

- The Moon sign (Chandra Rasi) is the zodiac sign where the Moon is positioned at the exact time of birth.
- It represents the manas (mind), emotional tendencies and psychological makeup.
- It is central to understanding the mind, emotions and inner nature.
- It is also called Chandra Lagna when the horoscope is analysed from the Moon's position, since the Moon rasi reflects the inner self, emotional responses and mental patterns.
- The Moon governs feelings, intuition and receptivity. The Moon rasi shows how a person experiences emotions and reacts to situations.
- Uses of the Moon sign in Jyotisha:
  - Transits of planets (Gochara) are traditionally assessed from the Moon.
  - Relationship compatibility analysis.
  - Religious practices (mantras, rituals, lifestyle adjustments) are based on the Moon rasi to stabilise the mind.

## 9. The Sun sign (Surya Lagna)

- The Sun sign (Surya Rasi) is the zodiac sign in which the Sun is placed at the time of birth.
- It is associated with the soul (atman), vitality and the deeper sense of life purpose.
- It represents the self, ego, willpower and the guiding principle of life.
- It is also called Surya Lagna when the horoscope is analysed from the Sun to assess inner drive, ambition and life purpose.
- It indicates core identity, ambition and the way a person seeks recognition.

## 10. Components of a lagna

Any lagna (Janma, Chandra or Surya) is a longitude (DD:MM:SS) that yields four components:

- Rasi components
  - Lagna rasi: the sign containing the point
  - Lagna lord (adhipati): the lord of that sign
- Nakshatra components
  - Lagna nakshatra: the nakshatra containing the point
  - Lagna nakshatra lord: the lord of that nakshatra
