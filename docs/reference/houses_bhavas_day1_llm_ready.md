## 1. Houses (bhavas)

- Houses are called bhavas in Sanskrit. Bhava means "state of being" or "condition of existence".
- They are twelve divisions of the celestial sphere.
- They are calculated from the Ascendant (Lagna), the sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. The Ascendant marks the beginning of the first house and anchors the chart, making it highly personal.

## 2. Signs versus houses

| Signs (rasis) | Houses (bhavas) |
|---|---|
| Fixed divisions of the zodiac, each spanning 30° | Divisions of the chart based on the Ascendant |
| Constant and universal, always in the same order (Aries to Pisces) | Relative: they shift according to the rising sign at birth |
| Show qualities and characteristics | Represent domains of life |
| Fixed, based on the Sun's annual path | Rotate with the Earth's 24-hour cycle, changing with time and place of birth |
| A planet in a sign expresses its nature through that sign's qualities | A planet in a house expresses its influence in that area of life |
| Example: Mars in Cancer takes on watery, emotional qualities | Example: Mars in the 4th house influences home, mother, property and inner peace |

In the natural zodiac (Kalapurusha Kundli), Aries is the 1st house, Taurus the 2nd, and so on to Pisces as the 12th.

## 3. House systems

Different mathematical methods divide the houses:
- Equal House system: the default in Vedic astrology
- Porphyry system
- Placidus system
- Whole Sign system

Why the Equal House system is used in Vedic astrology:
- It maintains the 30° division that matches the rasi system.
- It preserves symbolic meanings without distortion.
- It aligns with Parashara's principles of house signification.
- It reflects the orderly, mathematical nature of cosmic law (Rita).

Practical advantages:
- Works at all latitudes, including polar regions.
- Simple enough for manual computation.
- Gives consistent results across astrologers.
- Integrates seamlessly with the Vimshottari Dasha system.

## 7. Classification of houses

The seven classifications: Kendra, Succedent (Panapara), Cadent (Apoklima), Trikona, Upachaya, Dusthana and Maraka.

The four angles of the chart:

| Angle | Name | Position | House |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ascendant (ASC) | Lagna | Eastern horizon | 1st |
| Nadir | Imum Coeli (IC) | Beneath the sky | 4th |
| Descendant (DSC) | — | Western horizon | 7th |
| Zenith | Medium Coeli (MC), Midheaven | Overhead | 10th |

| Classification | Other names | Houses | Tamil term | Keynote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kendra | Angular, cardinal, quadrant | 1, 4, 7, 10 | Kentiram, Narkona Veedugal | "The four pillars" |
| Panapara | Succedent | 2, 5, 8, 11 | Panapara Veedugal | Houses of accumulation |
| Apoklima | Cadent | 3, 6, 9, 12 | Apoklimam Veedugal | Houses of falling away |
| Trikona | Trine | 1, 5, 9 | Trikona Veedugal | The auspicious triangle of dharma and fortune |
| Upachaya | Growth houses | 3, 6, 10, 11 | Upachaya Sthanangal, Valarchi Sthanangal | Improvement over time |
| Dusthana | Trika, challenging houses | 6, 8, 12 | Dusthana Sthanangal, Tunba Sthanangal | Growth through struggle |
| Maraka | Killer, death-inflicting houses | 2, 7 | — | Used in longevity analysis |

### 7.1 Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10)
- The angular houses form the foundation of the chart and anchor it.
- They are Vishnu Sthanas: they symbolise preservation, balance and protection.
- 1st house (Ascendant): self, body, life force.
- 4th house (IC): home, inner peace, mother.
- 7th house (Descendant): relationships, marriage, partnerships.
- 10th house (MC): career, karma, public role.

### 7.2 Panapara (2, 5, 8, 11)
- They represent stability, resources and consolidation: the houses of support.
- 2nd house: wealth, speech, family resources.
- 5th house: creativity, children, intelligence, past-life merit.
- 8th house: transformation, longevity, hidden matters.
- 11th house: gains, networks, aspirations.

### 7.3 Apoklima (3, 6, 9, 12)
- They represent transition, movement and dissolution. Their energy is less stable and more changeable, preparing for the next cycle: houses of transition, learning, preparation and release.
- 3rd house: skills, communication, effort — energy disperses into learning and action.
- 6th house: service, health, obstacles — energy dissolves into duties and challenges.
- 9th house: higher knowledge, dharma, journeys — energy shifts into philosophy and expansion.
- 12th house: moksha, losses, liberation — ultimate dissolution, release from material attachments.

### 7.4 Trikona (1, 5, 9)
- They represent dharma, fortune and blessings: the houses of blessings and spiritual upliftment.
- They are Lakshmi Sthanas: they bring fortune, merit and spiritual upliftment.
- 1st house (Ascendant): self, body, life path — the foundation of dharma.
- 5th house: creativity, children, intelligence, past-life merit (Purva Punya) — accumulated blessings and their expression.
- 9th house: higher knowledge, dharma, philosophy, long journeys — spiritual wisdom and expansion.

### 7.5 Upachaya (3, 6, 10, 11)
- Upachaya houses improve with time, effort and perseverance.
- 3rd house: effort, courage, siblings, communications — growth through efforts to build skills.
- 6th house: enemies, debts, disease, obstacles — growth through overcoming obstacles by discipline.
- 10th house: career, karma, authority — growth through hard work and responsibility, leading to recognition, status and sustained success.
- 11th house: gains, networks, fulfilment of desires — growth through social connections.

### 7.6 Dusthana (6, 8, 12)
- "Dusthana" comes from Sanskrit duh (bad) and sthana (place): bad or difficult houses.
- They are the houses of suffering and karmic purification.
- 6th house: enemies, debts, disease, obstacles.
- 8th house: sudden events, longevity, death, fear, hidden matters, transformation.
- 12th house: loss, expenditure, isolation, moksha, foreign lands.

### 7.7 Maraka (2, 7)
- Maraka means "killer". The Maraka houses are one of the factors in longevity analysis.
- The 8th is the house of longevity, and the 3rd is also a house of longevity, being the 8th from the 8th.
- The 2nd house (12th from the 3rd) and the 7th house (12th from the 8th) are the two principal Maraka houses.
- The lords of these houses also acquire the quality of giving or taking away life.
- Of the two, the 2nd house is the more powerful Maraka.
