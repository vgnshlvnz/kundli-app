# Navamsa (D-9) Calculator — Jyotisha Reference

Scope: lookup table mapping every 3°20' division of the zodiac to its nakshatra pada and its navamsa (D-9) sign.

How to use: find the planet's sign and its degree within that sign; the matching row gives the nakshatra pada and the navamsa sign in which to place the planet in the D-9 chart.

Conventions used in this file:
- Each sign has 9 amsas of 3°20'. The 108 amsas of the zodiac correspond one-to-one with the 108 nakshatra padas.
- Spans are degrees and minutes within the sign.
- Navamsa sign number: 1 = Aries through 12 = Pisces.
